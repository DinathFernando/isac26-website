I. THE CALLING

A stage for
extraordinary voices.

The Inter-School Announcing Competition (ISAC) is a trilingual student media tournament organised by the Ananda College Broadcasting Unit (ACBU), bringing together young communicators from schools across Sri Lanka.

From programme presenting and news reading to news editing, podcasting, dubbing, and sports commentary, ISAC celebrates creativity, confidence, clarity, and the craft of communication.
