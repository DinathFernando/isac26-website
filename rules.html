THE INSCRIPTIONS

Rules &
Regulations.

Every arena has its code. Read the official requirements carefully before preparing or submitting an entry to ISAC’26.

PLEASE READ BEFORE ENTERING

1. Eligibility

  • ISAC’26 is intended for eligible school students representing their schools, subject to the final eligibility criteria published by the organisers.
  • Contestants must select categories and age or school-level divisions for which they qualify.
  • School approval or verification may be required during registration.

2. Registration

  • Contestants must submit complete and accurate information through the official registration method announced by the organisers.
  • Each contestant is responsible for checking the category requirements, deadlines, formats, and submission instructions.
  • Do not submit passwords, payment information, or other sensitive details through unofficial links.

3. Originality and conduct

  • Entries must be the contestant’s own work unless a category’s published rules explicitly state otherwise.
  • Plagiarism, impersonation, fabricated credentials, or unauthorised use of another person’s work may result in disqualification.
  • Contestants must behave respectfully toward judges, organisers, other contestants, and participating schools.

4. Submission requirements

  • Follow the file format, duration, language, naming convention, and delivery method specified for each category.
  • Late, incomplete, corrupted, or incorrectly formatted submissions may not be accepted.
  • Keep a copy of your final submission until the competition concludes.

5. Judging and decisions

  • Entries will be evaluated against the criteria announced for their respective categories.
  • The organisers may request clarification or verification where necessary.
  • Decisions and procedures will follow the final rules issued by the ISAC’26 organising committee.

6. Media and publication

  • Where applicable, contestants and schools should ensure that they have permission to submit and publish any included third-party material.
  • Any use of contestant names, photographs, recordings, or submitted work for event coverage must follow the permissions and privacy terms communicated by the organisers.
Important: This page is a starter template, not a substitute for the official ISAC’26 rulebook. The organising committee must review, approve, and replace or amend these clauses before publication. Category-specific details, deadlines, age divisions, judging criteria, and submission limits have deliberately not been invented here.
